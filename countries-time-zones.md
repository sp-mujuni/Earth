# Countries of the World and Their Standard Time Zones

**195 countries** (193 UN member states + 2 UN observer states), same list as `countries.md`.

Notes:
- Offsets are **standard time** relative to UTC, with daylight saving time (DST) left out.
- Countries that span several zones show the range, with the main exceptions in brackets.
- Overseas territories are generally not included.
